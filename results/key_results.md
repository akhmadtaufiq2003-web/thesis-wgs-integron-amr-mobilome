## Interpretation

The strongest pooled signals involve total AMR burden, predicted plasmid-associated ARG burden, and total IntegronFinder structure burden. Complete-integron positivity and strict complete-integron ARG positivity were not significant after FDR correction. Predicted chromosome-associated ARG burden was similar between cohorts.

Because BioProject and cohort are fully aliased, these patterns should be described as cross-study/between-cohort differences rather than independent effects of invasive origin.
